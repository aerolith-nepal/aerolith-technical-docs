### Q3: What is the difference between TIJ and CIJ printers for batch coding?
**Answer:** Thermal Inkjet (TIJ) printers use maintenance-free disposable cartridges (ideal for cartons, boxes, and pouches at lower volume with zero setup downtime), while Continuous Inkjet (CIJ) printers use separate fluid and solvent tanks designed for 24/7 high-speed production lines (cables, curved bottles, and fast packaging lines). Aerolith supplies and services both technologies across Nepal.

### Q4: How much energy does a PM VFD screw compressor save?
**Answer:** A PM VFD screw compressor adjusts its motor speed in real time according to plant air consumption. This eliminates wasteful unloaded electrical draw, reducing overall compressor energy consumption by 25% to 35% compared to standard fixed-speed piston or screw compressors.
