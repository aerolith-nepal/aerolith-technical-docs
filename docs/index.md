## 1. Compressed Air Infrastructure

### A. PM VFD & Rotary Screw Air Compressors
Permanent Magnet Variable Frequency Drive (PM VFD) screw compressors regulate motor RPM according to real-time plant air demand, saving up to 35% electricity compared to fixed-speed units.
- **Operating Pressure:** 7.5 to 13 Bar
- **Preventive Maintenance:** Replace intake air filter and synthetic coolant every 2,500–3,000 running hours.

### B. Reciprocating Piston Compressors
Heavy-duty cast iron reciprocating piston units engineered for intermittent-duty workshops, automobile service centres, and medium-scale industrial fabrication.

### C. Air Treatment: Dryers, Filters & Air Receivers
- **Refrigerated Air Dryers:** Maintains a 3°C to 10°C pressure dew point to eliminate moisture.
- **Line Filters & Moisture Separators:** High-efficiency particle and oil removal down to 0.01 micron.
- **Air Receiver Tanks:** Certified vertical/horizontal pressure vessels for pulse dampening and peak storage.

## 2. Industrial Marking & Batch Coding Systems

### A. Laser Marking Machines (CO2, UV & Fiber)
Zero-consumable, permanent marking solutions engineered for 24/7 production lines:
- **Fiber Laser (20W / 30W / 50W):** 100,000+ hour source life for metals (SS, brass, aluminium) and rigid plastics.
- **CO2 Laser Marking:** High-speed coding on organic surfaces including paper, wood, glass, and cartons.
- **UV Laser Marking:** Cold marking technology with zero thermal impact for HDPE pharma packaging and sensitive polymers.

### B. Industrial Inkjet Printers (CIJ, TIJ & Handheld)
- **CIJ (Continuous Inkjet):** High-speed non-contact coding for round cables, curved beverage bottles, and pipes.
- **TIJ (Thermal Inkjet):** Maintenance-free 0.5-inch HP cartridge systems for crisp MRP, barcodes, and QR codes.
- **Handheld Portable Coders:** Flexible batch printing for cartons, bags, and outer sacks.
